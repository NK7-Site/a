Privacy Policy – NK7 GAME Data Protection

Last updated: November 26, 2026

1. Introduction

At NK7 GAME, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your data when you visit our website or use the NK7 GAME platform.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect and process the following categories of information:

  • Account Information: Email address, username, or login details you provide when creating or accessing an account.
  • Technical Data: Device type, operating system version, browser type, screen resolution, and basic connection logs.
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, navigation actions, session duration, and general feature usage.
  • Game Data: Game preferences, settings, and basic progress states where applicable.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for the following purposes:

  • Delivering and maintaining core website and app features
  • Improving performance, layout responsiveness, and user experience
  • Detecting abuse, preventing fraud, and keeping accounts secure
  • Responding to support requests and communicating important updates

4. Cookies & Local Storage

Our website and browser features may use local storage or essential cookies to remember interface preferences, maintain smooth navigation, and measure basic page performance. You can clear or manage browser storage anytime through your browser settings.

5. Data Security & Retention

We take practical security precautions to protect your information:

  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) for data transfer
  • Restricted access to administrative and operational tools
  • Periodic reviews of security practices and updates
  • Retention of data only for as long as needed for service operation or legal compliance

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable laws, you may request to:

  • Access or review personal data associated with your request
  • Request correction or deletion of inaccurate or unwanted data
  • Limit or object to certain non-essential data processing
  • Withdraw previously given consent where applicable

To submit a privacy request, contact us at privacy@nk7.com.

7. Third-Party Links

Our pages may link to external websites or services. We do not control the privacy policies or practices of third-party sites and encourage you to review their terms before sharing personal details.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect platform or legal updates. Any revisions will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or data handling on NK7 GAME:
