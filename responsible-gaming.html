Responsible Gaming – NK7 GAME Safe Play

Last updated: November 26, 2026

18+ This website and guide are intended for adults aged 18 and older.

1. Our Commitment

At NK7 GAME, we believe gaming should remain light, enjoyable, and balanced. We encourage healthy habits so players stay in control of their time and screen use.

2. Age Restriction

NK7 GAME is intended for users aged 18 and above. Parents and guardians should actively supervise device access and use parental controls where needed.

3. Practical Play Habits

Simple steps can help you keep gaming sessions healthy and well-paced:

  • Daily Time Limits: Decide how much time you want to spend before starting a session.
  • Regular Breaks: Step away from screens periodically to rest your eyes and mind.
  • Notification Control: Manage alerts so play does not interrupt sleep, work, or study.
  • Balanced Routine: Keep offline activities, rest, and daily duties as your top priority.

4. Know When to Step Back

Consider taking a break or stepping away if you notice any of these signs:

  • Gaming starts affecting work, study, sleep, or family commitments
  • You regularly lose track of time while playing
  • You spend more time or money than you planned
  • Gaming creates stress, frustration, or friction with others
  • You use play as a way to avoid everyday problems

5. Support & Help Resources

If you or someone you know needs guidance on healthy screen habits or gaming concerns, support resources are available:

  • India: Call 1800-11-4000 (National Helpline for Mental Health)
  • Global: Visit Gambling Therapy

6. Parental Guidance

Parents and guardians can protect younger users by:

  • Enabling operating-system screen time and app restrictions
  • Checking device usage and installed apps periodically
  • Talking openly about healthy digital and gaming boundaries
  • Keeping account credentials and device passcodes private

7. Policy Updates & Contact

We may update these Responsible Gaming guidelines occasionally as our platform evolves. Please also review our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions, or explore NK7 GAME Features.

For questions or support, email us at responsible@nk7.com.
