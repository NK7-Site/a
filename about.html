About NK7 GAME – Official Free Mobile Gaming Platform

Our Mission

NK7 GAME is dedicated to providing a free, lightweight, and secure mobile gaming experience for players across India and worldwide. We believe gaming should be accessible to everyone, regardless of device capability or data plan limitations.

What We Offer

  • Free to Play — No costs, no hidden fees, ever.
  • Lightweight — App is only ~5 MB, runs on Android 8.0+.
  • Secure Login — Encrypted accounts, 2FA support, no password sharing.
  • Curated Games — 50+ hand-picked titles from arcade to cricket.
  • Regular Updates — New games and features added monthly.

Our Team

Founded in 2026, NK7 GAME was created by a small team of developers and gaming enthusiasts who wanted to build a platform that prioritizes player safety, simplicity, and fun. Our team includes Android developers, security engineers, game curators, and support staff — all committed to providing the best possible mobile gaming experience.
