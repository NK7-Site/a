NK7 GAME Data Privacy & Security Policy for Players

Last updated: November 26, 2026

1. Overview & Our Privacy Commitment

At NK7 GAME, player trust and data transparency come first. This Privacy Policy explains clearly what information we collect when you browse our website or play mobile games on our platform, why we use it, and how you stay in control of your personal details.

2. Information We Collect

We keep data collection minimal and focused only on what is needed to run a fast, reliable gaming platform:

  • Account Information: Email address, username, or basic login credentials you voluntarily provide when creating or accessing an account.
  • Technical & Device Data: Device model, Android or OS version, browser type, screen resolution, and basic diagnostic logs to keep the ~5 MB app running smoothly.
  • Usage & Navigation Data: Pages visited, session duration, and feature interactions that help us improve layout speed and usability.
  • Gameplay Preferences: Saved game settings, language preferences, and progress states where supported.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information collected on NK7 GAME is used strictly for legitimate platform operations:

  • Delivering, maintaining, and updating core website and mobile game features
  • Optimizing page load speed, mobile responsiveness, and low-data performance
  • Detecting suspicious activity, preventing unauthorized access, and protecting player accounts
  • Responding to customer support inquiries and sharing essential service notices

4. Cookies & Browser Local Storage

Our website uses essential cookies and browser local storage to remember interface preferences, keep navigation smooth across pages, and measure basic site performance. We do not sell your browsing history, and you can clear or disable cookies anytime in your browser settings.

5. Data Security & Retention Practices

We apply practical technical and organizational safeguards to keep user information protected:

  • Encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections for all data transmitted between your browser and our servers
  • Strict access controls limiting operational tools to authorized team members
  • Routine security checks and software updates to reduce vulnerabilities
  • Limited data retention — information is kept only as long as necessary for platform functionality or legal obligations

6. Your Privacy Rights & Controls

You remain in control of your personal information. Depending on applicable data protection laws, you may request to:

  • Access a summary of personal information associated with your account or support request
  • Correct inaccurate account details or update your preferences
  • Request deletion of your personal data when no longer required for active services
  • Opt out of non-essential communications or withdraw previously granted consent

To submit a privacy request, email our team at privacy@nk7.com.

7. Children's Privacy

NK7 GAME is intended for users who meet the minimum legal age requirement in their region. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without verified parental or guardian consent.

8. Third-Party Links & External Services

Pages on this website may contain links to external downloads, partner pages, or third-party resources. Because external websites operate under their own privacy policies, we recommend reviewing their terms before submitting any personal details.

9. Policy Updates & Contact Details

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically as our platform evolves. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. For privacy questions, reach us at:

  • Privacy Team: privacy@nk7.com
  • Player Support: support@nk7.com
